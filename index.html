01 About

Thirty years of taking things apart to see how they work.... and then building better ones.

I'm a Principal Engineer at PrestonEastin, Inc. in Tulsa, Oklahoma, where I keep the engineering department, and a fair amount of the rest of the company, running faster. Over eight years there I've designed more than $15.2M of custom robotic handling equipment for welding applications, across 156+ unique pieces of engineered-to-order equipment.

Alongside the hardware, I've been writing software since a college elective called "VBA for Engineers" turned into a habit. In 2020 I founded a software company to build the tools I wished existed on the shop floor; starting with a faster way to search and cross-reference the thousands of CAD drawings most manufacturers are quietly buried in.
