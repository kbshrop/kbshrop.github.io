01 About

Thirty years of taking things apart to see how they work.... and then building better ones.

I'm a Principal Engineer at PrestonEastin in Tulsa. In eight years I've designed over $15.2M of custom robotic handling equipment for welding (around 156+ one-of-a-kind machines). Also made numerous niche software solutions over the years to speed up the boring parts of engineering, purchasing, receiving, finance, and production scheduling.

The software habit started with a college elective called "VBA for Engineers" and never left. In 2020 I founded Drawing Navigator to build the tools I wished existed on the shop floor, starting with a faster way to find your way through the thousands of CAD drawings most manufacturers are quietly buried in.

Outside of work, I make things nobody asked for: a clock that's only accurate to "-ish," a ruler that splits the difference between metric and imperial, and a kitchen timer for every burner. I'm an entrepreneur at heart and love to learn about topics I've never heard of before. Small personal projects are released under my brand: EngineeringJudgement.

I like stuff that looks like somebody cares. Somewhere along the way, we've traded curves, tapers, and a little theatrical flourish for straight lines, flat black rectangles, and whatever the cheapest extrusion allows. Great designers can make a lamp, a chair, or a house feel like it has a personality, and a good part of that is just not being afraid of a curve. I'd rather make something slightly more complicated and a lot more fun to look at. Nobody has ever been charmed by a gray box. I checked.
