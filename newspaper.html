Newspaper Edition

An engineering newspaper, printed on paper and delivered by mail. Remember how to fold one?

00Summary

Back to analog.

Everything I read about engineering lives on a screen, buried in a feed next to everything else. I wanted something you could hold, fold, and leave on a break room table.

The Newspaper Edition is a printed newspaper made of articles written by peers in the engineering community. Anyone can pitch an article, and anyone in the USA can request a copy.

Edition 0 went out by mail to 29 readers.

01Details

Each edition is built from reader-submitted articles. Authors pitch a piece by email, and readers sign up for a copy with their industry, job, and a USA mailing address.

Edition 0 had five pieces: a look at Slocum ocean gliders from Tony Kelbert, a case against undocumented scope creep from Sean Dotson, and three of my own on automating Excel with VBA, what "USA made" really means, and a preview of the sections still to come.

The readers are a mix: automation, biopharma, energy, manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, and aerospace. Job titles run from design and process engineers to founders, data scientists, recruiters, and business development managers.

Planned for future editions: comics, classifieds, datasets, reader statistics, and helpful hints and tips.
