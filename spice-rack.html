Home Project

Custom Spice Rack

A spice rack worthy of a home chef.

00Summary

8x the cost but I'm worth it

Great chefs organize their kitchens. This is a step towards a kitchen that reflects who I am as a chef.

I could have just bought a plastic spice rack, but they look cheap and feel flimsy. So I spent many hours and 8x the money to build the one I needed.

I'm a huge fan of starting to prototype with cardboard to get something you can feel and sense the right size. After that I got some sheet metal bent and suffered through stick-slip (again). Every time I work with plain bearings in a manual system I think I'll be okay.... and I'm wrong every time. So I threw out the plain bearings and mounted some ball bearing drawer slides that work fantastic.

I also got someone with a Universal Robotics cobot to help make a video which was a cool experience.

I've been using this for a few years now and it's still working great with no issues.
