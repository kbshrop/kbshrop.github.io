00Summary

A 24hour approximate clock

I like clocks. They are a mix between something mechanical and something useful. They can also be visually striking and unique. I almost never need the time down to the second, or even minute, so this is my first clock project to look into this concept.

ish is a 24-hour linear clock. Every hour of the day, 0 through 23, has its own vertical marker, and a row of minute lines at 0, 15, 30, 45 and 60 runs along the scale.

Each marker slides along the minute lines during its hour. When an hour hits 60 its marker drops down and the next one in line moves into place at 0.

Because the whole day is laid out at once, you see the current time as a position within the day, not just a pair of numbers. It only conveys rough time, readable to within about 5 to 10 minutes, hence the name.
